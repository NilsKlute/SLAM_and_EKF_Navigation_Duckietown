## Summary of Duckietown Software Terms of Use (Version 2.0)

This is an informal summary of the Terms of Use of Duckietown resources, including but not
limited to the Duckietown documentation, slides, software, simulation, evaluation
infrastructure and hardware (the "resources").

### 1. Personal use

You are welcome to use Duckietown resources for **personal educational** use.

We ask that if you disseminate video or photo content, such content must conform to the Media
Compliance Rule (see Appendix below).

### 2. Use for classes at accredited institutions

You may use Duckietown resources in **teaching classes at an accredited degree-granting
institution**, subject to the following conditions:

- a) We ask that you contact us and provide us with statistics about your class, so that we
  can keep a tally of the users of the Duckietown Platform around the world.
- b) We ask that the Duckietown Project is acknowledged on the class website, with a link to
  <https://duckietown.com>.
- c) Produced video or photo content follows the Media Compliance Rule (see Appendix).

Optionally, we would be thrilled if you join our international community of Duckietown
instructors. Please contact us at info@duckietown.com.

If you do intend to contact us with class statistics, or acknowledge Duckietown on your class
website, you are required to obtain a license agreement to use Duckietown resources in your
class. Please reach out to info@duckietown.com.

### 3. Use for research projects

You may use the Duckietown Platform in a research project, provided that:

- a) Duckietown is properly acknowledged in the paper: e.g. "The hardware/software used for
  the experiments was developed by, or built upon the resources developed by Duckietown ---
  https://duckietown.com."
- b) Produced video or photo content follow the Media Compliance Rule (see Appendix).

Optionally, we would appreciate if:

- a) You cite one of the papers that we wrote on the platform.
- b) You provide us with a copy of your paper.

### 4. Commercial uses

You may **not** use Duckietown resources in a commercial enterprise and you may not use the
name "Duckietown" or our logo connected to a commercial enterprise without written
authorization of Duckietown.

If you would like to use Duckietown resources in a commercial endeavor, you must contact us at
info@duckietown.com to arrange a licensing agreement. The revenue from these licensing
agreements will go to Duckietown in order to provide maintenance and technical improvements to
the resources, and to further disseminate the Duckietown Platform.

### 5. Contributions

We welcome patches and are happy to incorporate them. We ask that you assign the copyright to
us, so that we may release all software with the same license (this license) and it is easy
for us to enforce the guidelines above.

## Appendix — Media Compliance Rule for producing videos and other media

You are welcome to produce and distribute photos and videos of your Duckiebots, Duckiedrones,
and/or Duckietowns doing awesome stuff, provided that:

- a) proper attribution to the Duckietown Project is given in the photos and videos, with link
  to <https://duckietown.com>;
- b) the robots and the Duckietown Environment pictured are compliant with the Duckietown
  specifications as seen in the official appearance specifications.

What we want to avoid is that pictures of non-compliant robots or environments make life
difficult for others, as they might try to copy a wrong appearance.

In particular, note that a compliant Duckiebot has a duckie on top; and a compliant Duckietown
Environment has a minimum density of 3 duckies per square meter.

Therefore, it is absolutely forbidden to post videos of Duckiebots and Duckietown Environments
without duckies. We are happy to provide the duckies free of charge.
